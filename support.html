Scuff & Sons Support

Scuff & Sons is a puzzle game about getting furniture around tight corners.

Contact

For help, bug reports or suggestions, email scuff@aydho.com.

How to play

Drag a round handle to swing that end of the furniture around the other end. Drag the arrow handle, or the middle of the piece, to slide it. Hold both handles to slide and turn at once. Lift your fingers to set it down. The faint ghost on the rug shows where it goes and which way round: backs go against the wall.

Common questions

Why did they drop it? If both removalists end up at the same end, the load overbalances. Pick it up again and swing it so one stands at each end.

How do I get three stars? Deliver without a single scuff. Two stars allow two scuffs. Beating par earns an on-time bonus on the invoice.

How do I open more worlds? Earn stars. Each world needs a total number of stars across the earlier jobs.

When does the daily job change? At midnight in your time zone. Keep playing each day to build your streak.
