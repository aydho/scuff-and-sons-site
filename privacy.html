Scuff & Sons Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

Scuff & Sons is a puzzle game for iPhone. It runs entirely on your device. There is no Scuff & Sons account, server or backend.

What we collect

Nothing. Scuff & Sons does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Data that stays on your device

Your best runs, stars, earnings, overalls, daily streak and sound setting are kept on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game posts your daily job score and total earnings to Apple's Game Center leaderboards. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and the leaderboards show your Game Center nickname to other players. You can sign out of Game Center in Settings at any time and the game works the same without it.

Ratings and sharing

The game may occasionally ask you to rate it using Apple's standard rating prompt. If you share a daily result, the text you share is sent only where you choose to send it.

Children

Scuff & Sons is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.
